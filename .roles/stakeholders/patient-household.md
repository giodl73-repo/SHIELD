# Patient / Household

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the person or household trying to get care when geography, cost, language, transportation, or time off work may block access.

## Primary concerns

- Timely, nearby care and appointment availability (DIM-01).
- Affordability, coverage, and surprise or out-of-pocket cost (DIM-07).
- Referral continuity and not falling through handoffs (DIM-04).

## Used during

- Access, affordability, and referral-continuity scoring; tier/SLA for primary care, emergency access, and specialty referral.
