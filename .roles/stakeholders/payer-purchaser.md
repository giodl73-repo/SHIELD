# Payer / Purchaser

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures insurers, employers, public purchasers, and other payers deciding coverage, network design, and value of spend.

## Primary concerns

- Cost, coverage, and patient affordability (DIM-07).
- Benefit-cost and value of spend (DIM-12).
- Network adequacy, service breadth, and referral control (DIM-08/04).

## Used during

- Affordability, benefit-cost, service-breadth, and payer/consolidation scoring; tier/SLA feasibility review with the payer and consolidation realist.
