# Public-Health Agency

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures public-health agencies responsible for population outcomes, prevention, disease surveillance, and emergency preparedness.

## Primary concerns

- Population outcomes, prevention, and avoidable utilization (DIM-10).
- Surge readiness, redundancy, and coordination during emergencies (DIM-05).
- Disparities and community-level burden (DIM-11).

## Used during

- Public-health impact, resilience, and equity scoring; tier/SLA for surge and prevention-relevant service coverage.
