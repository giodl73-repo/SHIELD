# Frontline Clinician

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the clinicians and care teams who actually deliver visits, procedures, emergency care, and follow-up.

## Primary concerns

- Workload, staffing, burnout, and safety (DIM-06/03).
- Whether capacity is staffed and deliverable (DIM-02).
- Handoff quality, referral closure, and clinical risk (DIM-04).

## Used during

- Workforce, quality, capacity, and referral scoring; tier/SLA feasibility review with the clinician care-delivery lead.
