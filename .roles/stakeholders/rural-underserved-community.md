# Rural / Underserved Community

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures communities where hospital closure, thin primary care, specialty deserts, transportation barriers, or payer mix can make access fragile or absent.

## Primary concerns

- Having any local or reachable access at all (DIM-01/11).
- Provider shortages, service-line closure, and transfer dependence (DIM-06/08/04).
- Affordability and coverage barriers (DIM-07).

## Used during

- Equity, access, workforce, service-breadth, and resilience scoring; tier/SLA for local and regional access (T3/T4).
