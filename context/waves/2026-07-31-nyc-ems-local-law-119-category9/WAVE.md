# NYC EMS Local Law 119 Category 9 wave

Status: **complete**

## Deliverable

WP-021 captures the official, definition-compatible calendar-2025 Category 9
citywide and borough publication. It replaces neither the dispatch distribution
nor the MMR target context; it demonstrates why those differently defined
surfaces must remain separate.

## Fixed point

The executable fixture reconciles all borough incident counts to the citywide
total and preserves the official model measure. Validation requires the exact
numerator, whole-law reporting compliance, legal performance threshold,
pass/fail, dispatch comparison, causal, adequacy, candidate, and Taxlane fiscal
flags to remain false. Review details are in
`docs/vtrace/WORK_PACKAGE_NYC_EMS_LOCAL_LAW_119_CATEGORY9.md`.
