# SHIELD Open Adoption Guide

## Purpose

SHIELD is public and open to use as a reference model for aggregate,
evidence-gated healthcare-access analysis. It tests where service-network
methods transfer to care delivery and where they break.

Public use does not create a clinical study, licensing or accreditation
determination, Certificate-of-Need decision, payer decision, medical advice,
advocacy brief, or CMS, state-agency, health-system, payer, or provider
endorsement.

## Safe Adaptation Path

Use [`aggregate-adaptation-worksheet.md`](aggregate-adaptation-worksheet.md) to
scope one aggregate-only access, capacity, workforce, referral, affordability,
continuity, equity, or surge-resilience question.

Do not use patient records. Do not produce individual medical advice. Do not
turn transfer-strain evidence into a clinical recommendation.

## Contribution Targets

- aggregate source inventories for facilities, service lines, workforce,
  travel, referral, affordability, or surge evidence;
- privacy and public-language review;
- corrections to capacity, continuity, referral, equity, or affordability
  claims;
- reviewer notes from health planning, operations, payer, workforce, access,
  equity, emergency preparedness, or public-language lenses.

## Gate

Decision: **open_for_aggregate_reference_review**

Rationale: SHIELD can accept bounded aggregate source and review contributions.
Reuse alone does not create clinical, licensing, payer, Certificate-of-Need,
medical-advice, endorsement, or validation claims.
