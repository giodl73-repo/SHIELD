# SHIELD Public Access Claim Boundary

This boundary closes `SHIELD-PF-05`.

SHIELD aggregate baselines can be cited as reproducible source-grain evidence,
but they are not public healthcare-access findings by themselves. A CMS, USDA,
or HRSA baseline remains an aggregate-only denominator or registry result until
a claim-specific release record proves the larger interpretation.

## Promotion Rule

No SHIELD artifact may be promoted as a healthcare-access model, shortage map,
hospital-staffing finding, adequacy finding, intervention target, savings
claim, HLT admission, release-ready product, facility action, clinical action,
payer determination, Certificate-of-Need determination, accreditation or
licensing finding, medical advice, advocacy brief, or agency/health-system/
payer/provider endorsement unless the release record names:

- artifact path and regeneration command;
- source owner, vintage, row grain, residuals, and geography scale;
- demand basis, with `Surge` or `Baseline` explicit for capacity or adequacy;
- assessed and unassessed dimensions;
- allowed public claim and blocked public claims;
- every held interpretation for travel time, staffing, patient access,
  service-line availability, need, quality, outcomes, affordability, costs,
  savings, facility action, and clinical action;
- dispositions from the seven parliament roles;
- dispositions from the three editorial roles;
- downstream owner acceptance for the specific use.

If any field is missing, the artifact remains aggregate-only scoping evidence.

## Required Review Roles

Public-access promotion must record dispositions from Health-System Planner,
Clinician / Care-Delivery Lead, Operations & Capacity Officer, Health
Economist, Equity & Access Advocate, Public-Health & Prevention Advocate, Payer
& Consolidation Realist, Citation Auditor, Scope Keeper, and Numeracy Checker.

## Current Status

The CMS hospital footprint, CMS/USDA rurality distribution, HRSA primary-care
registry census, HRSA geography bridge, and HRSA designation-formula capacity
baseline are useful reproducible public-source baselines. They are not access,
staffing, shortage, adequacy, intervention, savings, HLT-admission, clinical,
facility, payer, or endorsement findings.

`REQ-009..011` remain `pass_with_risk` for public-access promotion until the
first promoted public claim records the boundary evidence and role dispositions
above.
